Privacy Policy

Coastal HTF Forecast (iOS) · Effective July 27, 2026

Summary: Coastal HTF Forecast does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Information We Collect

We collect nothing. The app has no sign-in, no registration, and no mechanism for sending your information to us or to anyone else. We operate no servers that receive data from the app.

Location

The app may ask permission to access your location while you are using it. This permission exists for a single purpose: so the map can display your position and let you center the view on your surroundings.

  • Your location is handled entirely on your device by Apple’s Maps framework.
  • The app never reads, records, stores, or transmits your coordinates.
  • Granting this permission is optional. The app is fully functional without it.
  • You can change or revoke it at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Data Stored on Your Device

Any locations you mark as favorites are saved locally on your device so they persist between sessions. This information never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Network Requests

To show current forecasts, the app downloads public coastal water level data files from GitHub Pages at ehsankahrizi.github.io. These are ordinary requests for publicly available files. No personal information is included in them.

As with any website visit, the hosting provider (GitHub) may record standard technical information such as IP address in its own server logs. This is handled under GitHub’s Privacy Statement and is not accessible to us in a form that identifies you.

The app also contains a link to the developer’s university lab website. That link opens only if you choose to tap it.

Third Parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics services, no crash reporting services, and no social media integrations. We do not sell, rent, or share information with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Children’s Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to ekahrizi@crimson.ua.edu.

Forecast data shown in the app is derived from the NOAA National Water Model. Coastal HTF Forecast is an independent research tool developed at the Coastal Hydrology Lab, University of Alabama. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NOAA or any government agency.
